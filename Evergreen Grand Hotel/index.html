Welcome to Evergreen Grand Hotel

Nestled in the heart of Osogbo, Evergreen Grand Hotel stands as a beacon of luxury and sophistication. Our establishment is dedicated to providing an exceptional hospitality experience that combines elegant accommodations with impeccable service.

Since our founding, we have been committed to creating a sanctuary where guests can escape the ordinary and immerse themselves in an atmosphere of refined comfort. Our meticulously designed spaces reflect our attention to detail and our passion for excellence.

At Evergreen Grand Hotel, we believe that true luxury lies in personalized service. Our dedicated team of professionals is trained to anticipate your needs and exceed your expectations, ensuring that every moment of your stay is memorable.

Whether you're visiting for business or leisure, our comprehensive amenities and strategic location make us the ideal choice for discerning travelers. From our gourmet dining options to our state-of-the-art facilities, every aspect of our hotel is designed with your comfort and convenience in mind.

We invite you to experience the distinctive blend of traditional hospitality and modern luxury that defines Evergreen Grand Hotel. Your journey to exceptional begins here.
